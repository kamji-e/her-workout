Thursday — Core + Back 🔥

Build a stronger core and a stronger back. You've got this! ❤️

🔥 Warm-Up — 8 Minutes

1. March in Place

March briskly to warm up your body.

2 minutes

2. Arm Circles

Make small circles, then gradually make them bigger.

30 seconds each direction

3. Standing Torso Rotations

Gently turn your upper body from side to side.

1 minute

4. Cat-Cow Stretch

On your hands and knees, gently round and arch your back.

1 minute

5. Shoulder Rolls

Roll your shoulders forward and backward.

1 minute

6. Gentle Standing Side Bends

Reach gently from side to side without forcing the stretch.

1 minute

💪🏽 Back Workout

1. Bent-Over Row

Hold your weights or water containers, hinge forward with a straight back, and pull toward your waist.

3 sets × 12 reps

2. Single-Arm Row

Support yourself with one hand and pull the weight toward your hip. Switch sides.

3 sets × 12 reps each side

3. Reverse Fly

With light weights, lean forward and slowly open your arms out to the sides.

3 sets × 12 reps

4. Superman Hold

Lie on your stomach and gently lift your arms and legs. Keep the movement comfortable.

3 sets × 20–30 seconds

🔥 Core Workout

1. Dead Bug

Lie on your back and slowly extend the opposite arm and leg while keeping your lower back steady.

3 sets × 10 reps each side

2. Reverse Crunch

Lie on your back and gently curl your knees toward your chest, lifting your hips slightly.

3 sets × 12 reps

3. Plank

Keep your body in a straight line. You can rest on your knees if needed.

3 sets × 30–60 seconds

4. Side Plank

Support yourself on one forearm and hold your body steady. Bend your knees for an easier version.

3 sets × 20–30 seconds each side

5. Leg Raises

Lie on your back and slowly raise and lower your legs. Bend your knees if needed, and stop if your lower back hurts.

3 sets × 10–12 reps

6. Mountain Climbers

From a high plank, bring one knee toward your chest, then alternate sides. Go slowly at first.

3 sets × 20 reps total

🧘🏽‍♀️ Cooldown — 5 Minutes

Gentle Back and Core Stretches

Try child's pose, a gentle seated twist, and slow breathing. Never force a stretch.

5 minutes

Thursday Complete! 🎉

You showed up for yourself today. Every workout is progress. ❤️
