WEDNESDAY WORKOUT

Hamstrings + Glutes 🍑

Your second major leg-building day. Focus on controlled movements and strong glutes.

01

WARM-UP

Get Your Body Ready

March or Jog in Place

2 minutes

Bodyweight Squats

15 reps

Hip Circles

30 seconds each direction

Alternating Reverse Lunges

10 each leg

Glute Bridges

15 reps

Leg Swings

30 seconds each leg

02

MAIN WORKOUT

Hamstrings + Glutes

Romanian Deadlift

4 sets × 10–12 reps

Hold your water container. Push your hips backward and lower it slowly while keeping your back neutral.

Hip Thrust

4 sets × 12–15 reps

Keep your feet planted and squeeze your glutes at the top. Add the container only if comfortable.

Bulgarian Split Squat

3 sets × 10 reps each leg

Use a sturdy chair or sofa for your rear foot. Hold a wall for balance if needed.

Single-Leg Glute Bridge

3 sets × 12 reps each leg

Keep your hips level and lift with control. Switch legs after each set.

Reverse Lunge

3 sets × 12 reps each leg

Step backward, lower under control, and push through your front foot to stand.

Frog Pumps

3 sets × 20 reps

Lie on your back with the soles of your feet together and gently lift your hips.
03

FINISHER

5-Minute Glute Challenge 🔥

Complete 3 rounds at a controlled pace:

  • 15 glute bridges
  • 15 squat pulses
  • 20-second glute bridge hold

Rest when needed. Keep your movements controlled rather than rushing through the rounds.

04

COOLDOWN

Stretch and Recover 🧘🏽‍♀️

Spend 5 minutes gently stretching your hamstrings, glutes and hips. Breathe slowly and avoid bouncing or forcing any stretch.

Another Day Stronger ❤️

You're building strength one session at a time. Celebrate your effort and give your body time to recover.
