🔥 Warm-Up — 8 Minutes

1. March or Jog in Place

Start slowly and gradually increase your pace.

2 minutes

2. Bodyweight Squats

Push your hips back, bend your knees, and stand tall again.

15 reps

3. Hip Circles

Place your hands on your hips and make controlled circles.

30 seconds each direction

4. Alternating Reverse Lunges

Step backward, lower gently, and return to standing. Alternate legs.

10 reps each leg

5. Glute Bridges

Lie on your back, bend your knees, and lift your hips.

15 reps

6. Leg Swings

Hold a wall for balance and gently swing one leg forward and backward.

30 seconds each leg

🍑 Full Lower Body Workout

1. Weighted Squats

Hold your weight close to your chest. Lower into a squat, then stand up steadily.

4 sets × 12 reps

2. Romanian Deadlifts

Hold your weights, soften your knees, push your hips backward, and stand tall again. Keep your back neutral.

3 sets × 12 reps

3. Bulgarian Split Squats

Place your back foot on a sturdy, low support. Lower slowly and push through your front foot. Use a wall for balance if needed.

3 sets × 10 reps each leg

4. Hip Thrusts

Rest your upper back against a stable support, if available. Lift your hips and squeeze your glutes at the top.

4 sets × 15 reps

5. Reverse Lunges

Step backward, lower both knees comfortably, then return to standing. Switch legs.

3 sets × 12 reps each leg

6. Sumo Squats

Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width and toes turned slightly outward. Lower slowly, then stand.

3 sets × 15 reps

🔥 The Friday Challenge — 5 Minutes

Complete the following circuit for as many comfortable rounds as you can in 5 minutes. Rest whenever you need to.

1. Squats

Complete 10 controlled squats.

10 reps

2. Reverse Lunges

Alternate legs, keeping your movements steady.

10 reps total

3. Glute Bridges

Lift your hips, squeeze your glutes, and lower with control.

15 reps

🧘🏽‍♀️ Cooldown — 5 Minutes

Stretch and Recover

Gently stretch your quads, hamstrings, glutes, and hips. Breathe slowly and never force a stretch.

5 minutes

You Finished the Week! 🎉❤️

Be proud of yourself for showing up. Rest, recover, and come back stronger.
