TUESDAY WORKOUT

Back + Core 💪🏽

Strengthen your back and build a strong, stable core — one exercise at a time.

01

WARM-UP

Get Your Body Ready

Arm Circles

30 seconds each direction

Shoulder Rolls

30–60 seconds

Cat-Cow

10 slow repetitions

Standing Torso Rotations

1 minute

Marching in Place

2 minutes

02

BACK

Build Strength 💪🏽

Bent-Over Water-Container Row

4 sets × 12 reps

Hinge at your hips, keep your back neutral, and pull the container toward your waist.

Single-Arm Row

3 sets × 12 reps each side

Support yourself on a sturdy surface and pull with control.

Reverse Fly

3 sets × 12–15 reps

Use a lighter container and move slowly.

Superman

3 sets × 12 reps

Lie on your stomach and gently lift your arms and legs without forcing your back.

Bird Dog

3 sets × 10 reps each side

From hands and knees, extend the opposite arm and leg while keeping your hips steady.
03

CORE

Build a Strong Centre 🔥

Dead Bug

3 sets × 10 reps each side

Keep your lower back gently against the floor as you extend opposite limbs.

Reverse Crunch

3 sets × 12 reps

Curl your pelvis gently upward instead of swinging your legs.

Plank

3 sets × 30–45 seconds

Keep your body in a straight line. Rest or use your knees if needed.

Heel Taps

3 sets × 20 total taps

Keep your shoulders slightly raised and move from side to side.
04

COOLDOWN

Stretch and Recover 🧘🏽‍♀️

Spend about 5 minutes stretching your back, shoulders and core gently. Breathe slowly and stop any stretch that causes pain.

You Did It! ❤️

Every rep is a step forward. Rest well, drink water, and be proud of yourself.
