MONDAY WORKOUT

Quads + Glutes 🍑

Build the front and side of your thighs while developing your glutes.

01

WARM-UP

Get Your Body Ready

March / Jog in Place

2 minutes

Bodyweight Squats

15 reps

Hip Circles

30 seconds each direction

Alternating Reverse Lunges

10 each leg

Glute Bridges

15 reps

Leg Swings

30 seconds each leg

02

MAIN WORKOUT

Build Strength

Weighted Squat

4 × 10–12

Hold your water container against your chest.

Bulgarian Split Squat

3 × 10 each leg

Use a sturdy chair or sofa behind you.

Reverse Lunges

3 × 12 each leg

Hold the water container when bodyweight becomes easy.

Sumo Squat

3 × 12–15

Wide stance, toes slightly outward.

Hip Thrust

4 × 12–15

Place the water container across your hips if comfortable.

Wall Sit

3 × 30–45 seconds

03

FINISHER

5-Minute Challenge 🔥

Complete 3 rounds:

  • 15 squat pulses
  • 10 jump squats
  • 20 alternating lunges
04

COOLDOWN

Take Your Time 🧘🏽‍♀️

Spend 5 minutes stretching your legs, hips and glutes. Breathe slowly and relax.

Workout Complete ❤️

You showed up. That's what matters. Come back tomorrow and keep going.
